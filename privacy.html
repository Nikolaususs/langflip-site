Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 8, 2026

LangFlip does not collect, store, transmit or share any personal data.

Keyboard input

To fix text typed in the wrong keyboard layout, LangFlip reads the keys you press and keeps the last few words in memory on your Mac. This buffer is never written to disk, never sent anywhere, and is cleared when you press Enter, click, or quit the app. Nothing is logged.

Clipboard

When you convert selected text, LangFlip briefly uses the clipboard and then restores its previous contents.

Network

LangFlip makes no network connections and contains no analytics, advertising or third-party SDKs.

Settings and dictionaries

Your preferences (shortcuts, chosen layouts, excluded apps, words never to fix) and any spelling dictionaries you add are stored locally on your Mac. Deleting the app removes them.

Children

LangFlip collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.

Политика конфиденциальности (кратко)

LangFlip не собирает, не хранит, не передаёт и не продаёт никакие персональные данные. Нажатые клавиши хранятся только в памяти Mac для исправления последних слов, не записываются на диск и никуда не отправляются. Приложение не выходит в интернет и не содержит аналитики и рекламы. Настройки и добавленные словари хранятся только на вашем Mac. Связь: devesp2030@gmail.com
